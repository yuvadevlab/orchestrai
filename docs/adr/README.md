## ADR Template

```markdown
# ADR-XXX: [Short title of solved problem and solution]

## Status

[Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Deprecated]

## Context

What is the context, requirement, or architectural challenge?

## Decision

What is the chosen approach or design?

## Alternatives Considered

- Alternative A: Pros / Cons
- Alternative B: Pros / Cons

## Consequences

### Positive

- Benefit 1
- Benefit 2

### Negative / Trade-offs

- Trade-off 1
- Mitigation strategy
```
